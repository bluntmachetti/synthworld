# Conformance and generalisation

Shipped reference benchmarks are inspectable conformance fixtures. Their evaluator
truth may be public, so they test contract integration, replay, and scoring behavior;
they do not prove blind generalisation.

Generated tiers vary explicit inputs under published rules. They still do not become
held-out merely because a profile is named `held_out`. Sensitivity, custody, profile
name, publication lifecycle, and capability maturity are separate dimensions.

Make claims at the level the evidence supports: reporting accuracy is not deployed
enforcement, a design-intent trace is not a product measurement, and a frozen smoke
fixture is not enterprise-scale evidence.
